There's a method
to the “what if”.

The ideas can be silly.
The care that goes into them isn't.

Usually, something small bugs me. Too many tabs. Food forgotten in the fridge. A tool that asks too much of someone who's already overwhelmed.

So I follow the thread: Who is this for? What would actually help? What's the smallest thing worth building? Then I make it, use it, and find out where I was wrong.

AI helps me move from “hmm” to “here, try this.” The curiosity, product decisions, and wildly specific problems? Those are mine.

Vibecoded. Not vibe-decided.

Stay curious.
Make the weird thing.

Your next rabbit hole might be a really good idea.
